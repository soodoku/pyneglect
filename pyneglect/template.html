How this list works

The directory requires an available, non-archived, non-disabled, non-mirror GitHub repository with issues enabled, an open issue updated within 365 days, and a push within 365 days. Forks and corporate projects remain eligible. The shortlist takes the 25 most downloaded eligible projects with an open issue updated within 180 days. Filters apply to the selected view.

Downloads are for the repository’s largest package, not a sum or a count of users. CI, mirrors, and caches affect this measure. Sibling packages are listed with each project. Contributor counts are past-year human commit-author identities reported by ecosyste.ms; they are not maintainer counts. Data last synced more than 90 days before collection is shown as unknown. A contributor limit excludes unknown values.

Issue updates and pushes may come from users or bots. They do not establish maintainer engagement, issue difficulty, or willingness to accept a contribution. Examples show the five most recently updated open issues; their first 20 labels and first 10 assignees are displayed. Label filters check the repository’s open issues for the exact labels “good first issue”, “good-first-issue”, “help wanted”, and “help-wanted”.
